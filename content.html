About this test site

This is the DMW SiteApp test of the JohnBaluka.com site, published by the DMW Site Designer to test.johnbaluka.com.

It exists to check three things before the dev site is published with the SiteApp:

  1. The slides pages, published from their decks as before.
  2. Interactive slides (PowerPoint's Copilot interactive slides), each running in a sandboxed frame.
  3. A SiteApp-only page: this one, written in Markdown and published as content.html into a data repository of its own.
